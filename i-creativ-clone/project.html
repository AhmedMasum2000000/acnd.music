Global Vision Rebrand

Year

2024

Services

Identity, Web

Project Hero Image

The Challenge

Our client, a technology consultancy with a strong market presence but a dated visual identity, approached us to create a comprehensive rebranding that would position them as innovative leaders while maintaining their credibility with established clients.

Our Approach

We conducted interviews with clients, prospects, and the leadership team to understand the true brand promise. What emerged was a story of bringing order to complexity — turning data into insight, strategy into results.

The Solution

A comprehensive identity system built on geometric principles that suggest both logic and creativity. The wordmark uses a custom typeface modification. The color palette bridges the technical (cool grays and blues) with human warmth (amber and rose). Every application, from business cards to the website, reinforces the core message.

The Results

The rebrand successfully repositioned the client. Within six months of launch, they reported increased lead quality and improved conversion rates. The identity became a recruiting tool — top talent wanted to work for a brand that looked and felt modern.
