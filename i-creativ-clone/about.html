How it started

Meridian was founded in 2006 by two people who had spent long enough at larger agencies to know what they wanted to do differently. The plan was modest: take on fewer projects, do all the work ourselves, and stop when the work stopped being good.

Nearly twenty years later the plan has not changed. We have made sites for museums, foundries, architects, energy companies and a radio station, and the studio is still two people.

What we hold to

01

Truth

We show real content at the first design review. Placeholder text hides problems, and every problem it hides gets more expensive the later it surfaces.

02

Simplicity

The best version of most features is the one we talked you out of. What remains should be obvious enough to need no explanation.

03

Creativity

Craft is not decoration. A site earns attention by being unmistakably itself, and that comes from judgement applied consistently, not effects applied liberally.
