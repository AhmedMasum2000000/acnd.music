Before you book

Straight answers, no rate card

Twenty questions we get asked every week, answered properly — what it costs and why nobody publishes a rate, which format does which job, and how to tell whether any of it worked.

Read the answers
  • How much does billboard advertising cost in Bangladesh? There is no single rate — digital is sold by the minute per day, so the site, the size and the run all move the number.
  • Digital or static — which is better? Neither. Static owns a face for a season; digital changes the same day and targets the hour.
  • Where are the best billboard sites in Dhaka? The slowest traffic, not the most. Gulshan, Mohakhali, Karwan Bazar and the junctions that queue.
  • How do you actually book one? Four things — cities, dates, format, budget band — and a written plan comes back inside a working day.
