4.1Company profile

An advertising company that also happens to be a manufacturer.

Ad Pro Communications Ltd. is a Bangladeshi out-of-home advertising company incorporated in Dhaka on 31 October 2019. We plan, own, build, install and report outdoor media. 4.4Government liaison and permissions

The part of this contract that goes wrong is the part we do in-house.

Unauthorised branding in Bangladesh is not a paperwork problem. It is a removal, a fine, a photograph of a competitor's hoarding where yours used to be, and a conversation with a city corporation that then remembers your brand. That is the risk this RFP is really buying against, and it is the reason permission capability is weighted more heavily here than execution or price.

AD PRO does not subcontract this. We maintain a standing liaison desk that files in our own name, because every one of our fifty-eight sites is held under permissions we obtained and renew ourselves. The same desk, the same people and the same relationships would carry Uber's filings.

4.8How a campaign runs

From brief to closure report, with a gate Uber controls.

  • Brief and site surveyUber gives the city, the dates and the objective. We survey candidate sites and return photographs, exact dimensions, facing, traffic context and the permitting authority for each.
  • Permission filingFiled in AD PRO's name within 48 hours of site approval. Government and authority fees are quoted at face value with the receipt to follow.
  • Uber's written approval, the gateUber approves the site, the artwork and the cost in writing. Nothing is produced or installed before this, and nothing is installed before the external permission is also in hand. Both are held on file.
  • ProductionPrinting, fabrication and pre-flight against Uber's brand guidelines in our own facility. Proof to Uber within 48 hours of final artwork.
  • InstallationOur crews, our safety method statement, road clearance where needed. Installed inside the agreed campaign window.
  • Proof of display within 24 hoursDated, geo-referenced photographs of every site; played-spot logs for digital screens.
  • Monitoring and maintenanceRoutine checks by the divisional executive. Damaged, incorrect or non-functioning material attended within 24 hours and replaced at our cost where the fault is ours.
  • Removal, restoration and closureBranding removed and the site restored at campaign end. Closure report with photographs, permission documents and full cost reconciliation against every purchase order.

AD PRO Communications Ltd. undertakes that all government fees, media-owner charges and third-party costs incurred on Uber Bangladesh's behalf will be billed to Uber as pass-through actuals at zero markup, each supported by the corresponding quotation, invoice or official receipt.

No handling fee, markup or commission is proposed anywhere in this response. Should any circumstance ever warrant one, AD PRO will state the reason in writing, provide documentation showing the cost break-up, and obtain Uber's prior written agreement before the cost is incurred, as Part 3 of the RFP requires.

On the fifty-eight LED screens AD PRO owns and operates there is no third-party cost to pass through at all. Uber is quoted the operator's rate directly.
