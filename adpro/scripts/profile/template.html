04 Who we are

An advertising company that also happens to be a manufacturer.

AD PRO Communications Ltd. was founded in 2017 by a group of outdoor professionals who had spent their careers planning and buying media for Bangladesh’s largest advertisers — and who were tired of the gap between what was promised and what actually went up.

Today we are an advertising solution provider with unified expertise across LED and static outdoor, print media, printing and packaging, indoor and outdoor branding, software, events and activation, logistics and digital marketing. Our clients range from the country’s heaviest out-of-home spenders to first-time advertisers.

Our mission is to keep our clients competitive with marketing that is proven rather than fashionable, and to make every encounter a customer has with your brand a good one.

Our vision is simpler still: increase the return our clients get on what they spend, and raise the standard of outdoor advertising in Bangladesh while we do it.

07 The LED network

Fifty-eight screens, in the places the country actually stops.

Our network is planned around traffic, not around what happens to be vacant. The sites sit at the junctions where Dhaka, Chattogram, Sylhet, Cox’s Bazar, Rajshahi, Rangpur, Bogura, Cumilla, Feni and Narayanganj slow down — because a screen that is read for forty seconds in stopped traffic is worth more than one glimpsed at speed.

Every site has its own page with the photograph, the exact dimensions, the pixel pitch, the resolution, the facing and the on-air hours. Digital is sold by the minute per day with a minimum of 60 minutes, and every campaign closes with a played-spot log.

08 How we work

Brief to close report, in four steps.

  • 01 Tell us the city, the dates and roughly the budget Four pieces of information are enough to start. You do not need a media plan to talk to us — that is what we are for.
