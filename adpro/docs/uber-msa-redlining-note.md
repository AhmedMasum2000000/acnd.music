# Uber MSA + SOW — contract review

**For:** ABM Zakaria Habib, Chief Executive Officer
**Re:** Uber Bangladesh Limited standard Services Agreement and Statement of Work
**Date:** 25 August 2026

## Recommendation

**Accept the template without redlines**, and say so in the RFP response.

Uber has stated the template is mandatory, that vendor templates will not be
accepted, and that it expects "no to minimal" redlining to keep contracting fast.
Contract speed is not scored directly, but it is the last gate before award, and
being the one bidder who does not send the document to a lawyer is worth more
here than any clause we would realistically win.

The clauses below are the ones that carry real exposure. None of them is unusual
for a company of Uber's size, and none is worth losing position over — but they
should be a decision rather than an oversight, and three of them change how the
account must be run day to day.

## What we are accepting

### 1. Uncapped indemnity, against a capped Uber liability

Clause 8. AD PRO indemnifies Uber, its affiliates, officers and agents in full.
Uber's own liability excludes indirect, punitive, incidental and consequential
loss and is capped.

*Why it is acceptable:* the exposure this creates is almost entirely the exposure
we already carry — a fall from height, an unpermitted installation, a structure
that fails. It is managed by doing the work properly, not by drafting.

*What it means operationally:* the two-key rule in the proposal (Uber's written
approval **and** the external permission, both on file, before any fixing) stops
being good practice and becomes the thing standing between us and an uncapped
claim. It must not be waived for a campaign date, ever, by anyone.

### 2. Termination for convenience, at Uber's sole discretion

Clause 9. Uber may terminate all or part of the agreement or any SOW at any time,
on written notice.

*Why it is acceptable:* standard for this counterparty, and the one-year term is
a framework rather than a volume commitment either way.

*What it means operationally:* do not fund long-lead inventory or fabricate
against expected Uber volume without a purchase order in hand. Every cost we
incur should be traceable to a PO — which is also what the pass-through model
requires.

### 3. No publicity without written consent

Clause 6. We may not advertise or announce that Uber is a client without prior
written consent.

*Why it matters more to us than to most vendors:* case studies are how this
business sells, and a live Uber campaign is the most visible work we would do all
year.

*Action, separate from the contract:* once the agreement is signed, ask Rawnak
Zarin for written consent to name Uber as a client and to use campaign
photography in credentials. Ask for it as a normal marketing courtesy, not as a
contract amendment — it costs Uber nothing to grant and is far easier to obtain
than a redline.

### 4. Subcontractors need prior written approval

Clause 2. Any subcontractor requires Uber's prior written consent, and we remain
fully responsible for them.

*What it means operationally:* the divisional riggers and crews outside Dhaka
must be scheduled and approved in advance, not engaged the week of installation.
Submit the standing list of contracted crews for the nine cities outside Dhaka
for blanket approval at onboarding, rather than seeking consent site by site.

### 5. FCPA and anti-bribery representations

Clause 4. We represent that no gratuity has been or will be offered, and
acknowledge the US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act.

*What it means operationally:* this bears directly on the permission work, which
is the largest single thing we are being bought for. Every authority fee is paid
at the official rate against an official receipt, and the receipt goes in the
evidence pack. No facilitation payment is made, and none would be reimbursable.
Brief the liaison desk on this specifically before the first filing.

### 6. Records and audit

Clause 3. Uber may inspect records of time, work and charges.

*Why it is acceptable:* it is the same discipline the pass-through model already
demands, and we have public-sector clients who audit us today.

## SOW Exhibit A

The Statement of Work is a blank template. Every substantive field —
summary of services, detailed description, requirements, schedule, reporting,
fees and payment schedule — is drafted by us and agreed with Uber.

**Suggested next step:** rather than wait to be asked, offer a completed draft
SOW built from the accepted proposal. It costs a day, it removes a round trip
from contracting, and it means the scope on the contract is the scope we wrote.
