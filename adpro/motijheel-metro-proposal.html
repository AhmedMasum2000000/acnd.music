01The station

One station, every screen in it.

Motijheel is Dhaka's commercial and banking centre: the central bank, the head offices of most of the country's banks and insurers, and the offices that serve them. Its metro station brings in the people who work there, every working day, and sends them home again.

The station carries 28 LED screens: two large landscape screens on the concourse walls and 26 standard portrait screens on the columns along the passenger routes. Book the screens you need, or take the whole station at the package rate.
