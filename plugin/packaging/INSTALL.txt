ACND OBSERVATEUR
Free, forever. Made by ACND.

Drive, space and width in one gesture: warm saturation, a deep stereo
reverb and a width control, built to sit on anything from a vocal to a
full mix bus.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
INSTALL
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Copy the formats your DAW uses into the folders below, then rescan
plugins in your DAW (or restart it).

WINDOWS
  ACND Observateur.vst3   ->  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
  ACND Observateur.clap   ->  C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP\
  ACND Observateur.exe    ->  anywhere (standalone app, no DAW needed)

MACOS
  ACND Observateur.vst3       ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
  ACND Observateur.component  ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/   (Logic, GarageBand)
  ACND Observateur.clap       ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/
  ACND Observateur.app        ->  /Applications/   (standalone app)

  If macOS says the plugin "can't be opened" or your DAW skips it, open
  Terminal and run:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"ACND Observateur.vst3"
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"ACND Observateur.component"
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/"ACND Observateur.clap"

LINUX
  ACND Observateur.vst3  ->  ~/.vst3/
  ACND Observateur.clap  ->  ~/.clap/
  ACND Observateur.lv2   ->  ~/.lv2/

------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  DRIVE   Warm, tape-like saturation. 0 adds none at all.
  SPACE   How much of the reverb you hear. The big one.
  SIZE    How long the reverb rings, from a small room to near-infinite.
  TONE    Darker or brighter reverb.
  WIDTH   0% mono, 100% as it was, 200% extra wide.
  OUT     Output level.

  Drag up/down to turn a knob. Double-click to reset it.
  Hold Ctrl (Windows/Linux) or Cmd (macOS) while dragging for fine control.
  Drag the window corner to resize.
